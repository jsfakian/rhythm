About RHYTHM

From clinical questions to usable evidence.

RHYTHM (Radiation, Health, Safety & Quality for Youth) is a four-year EU4Health project on safer, optimised medical imaging for children and young adults. The University of Crete leads Work Package 5, which builds the European CT dose and image repository, and contributes to data governance in WP1.

For partner institutions

The RHYTHM upload platform.

Partner hospitals register their CT scanners and protocols, enter examination dose data and submit anonymised image study sets. Every file is checked against a GDPR-strict anonymisation rule set before it is stored, and the server, never the partner, assigns the Repository Study ID.
